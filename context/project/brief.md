# Project brief

## Who is this for?

Two audiences of equal weight:

- **Fellow designers**: colleagues, former colleagues, future colleagues and peers. The site should inspire them and bring Paul's work to their attention.
- **A possible new employer**: someone interested in Paul's work so far and in his reflections.

Neither group comes first. The employer also cares about the story behind the work and the inspiration, because that shows what kind of designer Paul is and where his strength lies. So the stories, talks and publications are not secondary to the CV and projects.

## What problem do we solve?

Paul is leaving Fresk Digital, the company he co-founded, and is taking a new step in his career. New opportunities are coming, and the existing portfolio site needs to be ready for them. That means two things:

- **Content**: check that everything is correct and up to date.
- **Form**: sharpen the design again.

This kit exists so the site can be maintained well from here on.
