# Project brief

## Who is this for?

Two audiences of equal weight:

- **Fellow designers**: colleagues, former colleagues, future colleagues and peers. The site should inspire them and bring Paul's work to their attention.
- **A possible new employer**: someone interested in Paul's work so far and in his reflections.

Neither group comes first. The employer also cares about the story behind the work and the inspiration, because that shows what kind of designer Paul is and where his strength lies. So the stories, talks and publications are not secondary to the CV and projects.

## What problem do we solve?

Paul is leaving Fresk Digital, the company he co-founded, and is taking a new step in his career. New opportunities are coming, and the existing portfolio site needs to be ready for them. That means two things:

- **Content**: check that everything is correct and up to date.
- **Form**: sharpen the design again.

This kit exists so the site can be maintained well from here on.

## What is in scope, and what is not?

In scope: the existing sections, checked on content and sharpened in form.

- CV, including a version suited for sending as a PDF or linking to
- Selected projects: Paul's experience and where his love for the craft lies
- Publications, with links to read them
- Talks: the occasion and what each talk was about
- Stories: things Paul came across as a designer that he wonders about, is surprised by or admires

Not in scope now: new sections. The site keeps its current set of sections for this upgrade.

Later: **Inspiration**, a section Paul has already started on. It is a curated collection of files, inspiration and links he came across: not work he made, but work he values, has curated and wants to share. It will be added after this upgrade.

## How do we know it worked? (success criteria)

The upgrade worked when:

- **Paul trusts the content.** Everything is correct and up to date. The CMS manages all content; that is the starting point (whether it does so today still needs checking).
- **It shows Paul understands digital.** He will send it to a digital agency, so the readers know the craft. It does not have to be the most beautiful site, but it must work well, feel current and feel like his own.
- **The details are right.** Elements are aligned and everything scales properly across screen sizes. Nothing sits slightly off anymore.
- **Peers see and feel the care.** It is clear time went into it, and there is an interesting gimmick to discover, such as each story's unique colour derived from its title.
